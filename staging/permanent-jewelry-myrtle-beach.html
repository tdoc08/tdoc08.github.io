Whether you're celebrating a milestone, treating yourself, or giving someone a memory they'll wear forever, we make the experience as unforgettable as the jewelry itself.

Common Questions

Permanent Jewelry
FAQ

Everything visitors ask before booking at our Tanger Outlets Myrtle Beach studio.

Where in Myrtle Beach is Evermore Permanent Jewelry?

We're at Tanger Outlets on Highway 17 — Suite 215, 10835 Kings Rd, Myrtle Beach, SC 29572. The studio is in the main outlet promenade with easy parking nearby.

Do you take walk-ins?

Yes — walk-ins are welcome whenever a welder is free. We recommend booking online to guarantee a slot, especially on Friday and Saturday during peak vacation season.

Is permanent jewelry painful?

Not at all. The micro-weld closes a tiny jump ring on the chain — there's a brief flash but nothing touches your skin. Most clients describe it as completely painless.

Can I shower, swim, and sleep in permanent jewelry?

Yes — that's the whole point. Our 14k gold filled and sterling silver chains are built for daily wear, including ocean swimming, showers, and sleep. They're hypoallergenic and tarnish-resistant.

How much does permanent jewelry cost in Myrtle Beach?

Bracelets start at $85 (sterling silver) or $95 (14k gold filled). Anklets $100 / $110. Necklaces $150 / $160. Hand chains $170 / $190. Solid gold pieces are quoted at appointment based on the day's market rate.

Can I host a bachelorette or birthday party at your Myrtle Beach studio?

Yes. We host private parties at the Tanger Outlets studio and we travel to your venue throughout the Grand Strand — Murrells Inlet, Pawleys Island, North Myrtle Beach, Conway, and beyond. See private events for group pricing.

How long does a permanent jewelry appointment take?

About 20-30 minutes per piece. Group appointments scale based on size — a party of 6 typically takes 60-90 minutes start to finish.

Can I get permanent jewelry while on vacation in Myrtle Beach?

Absolutely — we get tons of vacation guests. Walk-ins welcome at our Tanger Outlets studio. Book online if you want a guaranteed slot during peak season (June-August and Thanksgiving / Christmas weeks).
