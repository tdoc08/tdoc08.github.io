The Grand Strand Guide

Myrtle
Beach

Myrtle Beach anchors the Grand Strand — a 60-mile stretch of Atlantic coastline that runs from Little River down to Pawleys Island. It's a place that wears two faces beautifully: family-friendly entertainment along the Boardwalk and Broadway at the Beach, and quieter, low-country charm just a few miles south in Murrells Inlet, Pawleys Island, and beyond. Whether you're here for a long weekend or settling in for the season, there's far more to discover than the strip suggests.

The Beaches

Sun &
Sand

Myrtle Beach Boardwalk

The 1.2-mile oceanfront promenade is the heart of the beach scene — restaurants, shops, the SkyWheel, and direct sand access. Sunrise here is unforgettable, and the energy after dark feels like a small carnival.

North Myrtle Beach (15 min north)

Quieter, more family-friendly stretches of sand with the same Atlantic but a fraction of the crowd. Cherry Grove and Ocean Drive are the picks — wide, clean, and great for a peaceful morning walk.

Pawleys Island (30 min south)

One of the oldest summer resort communities on the East Coast — historic beach cottages, almost no commercial development, and a slow, low-country pace that feels worlds away from the Grand Strand strip.

Attractions & Entertainment

Iconic
Stops

SkyWheel Myrtle Beach

The 187-foot oceanfront observation wheel is the city's most photographed landmark. Climate-controlled glass gondolas, sweeping Atlantic views, and an unbeatable sunset ride.

Brookgreen Gardens

9,100 acres of sculpture gardens, native wildlife preserves, and historic rice plantation grounds in nearby Murrells Inlet. Home to the largest collection of American figurative sculpture in the country — easily a half-day visit.

Broadway at the Beach

An open-air entertainment complex with restaurants, shops, Ripley's Aquarium, and live music. The lake-side dining at sunset is a Myrtle Beach classic.

Dining & Day Trips

Explore
More

Murrells Inlet MarshWalk (20 min south)

A half-mile wooden boardwalk along a saltwater estuary, lined with seafood restaurants, live music, and dolphin-spotting decks. The original "Seafood Capital of South Carolina" — and the best sunset spot for miles.

Tanger Outlets Myrtle Beach

An outdoor outlet center with 100+ stores — and our Evermore studio in Suite 215. Great for a full shopping day that starts or ends with permanent jewelry.

Conway Riverwalk (20 min west)

A historic Lowcountry town built along the Waccamaw River. Spanish moss, antique shops, and excellent locally-owned restaurants. A welcome change of pace from the strip.

Charleston, SC (90 min south)

One of America's most beautiful historic cities — cobblestone streets, antebellum architecture, and exceptional cuisine. A perfect overnight or long day trip from Myrtle Beach.

Wilmington, NC (75 min north)

A charming riverfront historic district, beautiful beaches at Wrightsville and Carolina Beach, and a thriving arts scene. An easy day trip across the state line.
